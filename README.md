# SSC-Research-Evidence

Public-facing evidence catalogue and, after governed review, a version-controlled archive for the **Scale Space (SS)** and **Complex Numeric Representational System (CNRS)** research programme.

Research programme directed by Donald G. Palmer (ORCID [0000-0003-4335-5533](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-4335-5533)).

## Current status

**Repository initialized — no working-note evidence has been released.** The [Chapter 15 public inventory](registers/TC_CH15_WN_catalogue_v0.md) lists provisional result and working-note identifiers for transparency; catalogue entries are not independently verified findings, downloadable working notes, or claims of public availability. No research source bundles, test artifacts or release tags have been published here.

## Scope and limits

This repository is designed to make calculations and supporting material inspectable, including corrections, contrary results and unresolved questions. The underlying SSC programme is research in progress. **A repository entry is not external peer review, experimental confirmation, an endorsed scientific theory or a guarantee of reproducibility.**

The commercial book manuscripts are not licensed or distributed through this repository. Third-party publications and figures are not republished here without permission.

See [publication and licensing policy](docs/PUBLICATION_AND_LICENSES.md) before reuse: this repository does **not** yet grant an open-content or software license.
