# Technical Companion Chapter 15 — public evidence inventory (catalogue v0)

**Status: catalogue only. No WN source documents or verification data have been released in this repository.**

This catalogue is adapted from the SSC working register `TC_ch15_source_index_v2.md` (19 November 2026), which remains an internal provenance record. It provides 44 numbered working-note identities plus one character-repaired companion, WN-26R. The unreleased notes concern TC Chapter 15's provisional results U1–U18. Titles and research status are listed for transparency; the documents, calculations, code and their completeness have **not** been independently audited or published here.

**Important:** These entries are *not* verified scientific findings, nor proof that a source is public. Roles include corrections, counter-results, author decisions and provisional calculations. `WN-26R` is a character-repaired rendering of `WN-26`, not a second derivation. Some earlier results were superseded or held out. Further technical review is required before depositing the actual sources.

## Access, exact identities, and future releases

The original filenames, internal Dropbox IDs, internal paths, sizes and content hashes are maintained in SSC's governed **internal** source register. They are deliberately not reproduced here because those file locators are not public evidence URLs. No private Dropbox links or permission claims are implied. Once an individual note is approved for release, its versioned public source file, correction history and verifiers should be linked to its WN entry at a *specific GitHub release commit* (and DOI when available).

Until then, cite the scientific result as a **provisional claim in the Technical Companion**, not as a verified public WN source. Public release requires an evidence review, permission and provenance check, and a maintained register-to-file integrity mapping.
