# Release v1.0.0: Technical Companion Chapter 15 evidence notes

**Tag:** `v1.0.0` (the release commit is the one this tag points to)

**Contents**

- 44 public editions, WN-01 to WN-44, in `tc/ch15/notes/`: the unpublished working notes cited by *The Forgotten Coordinate: Technical Companion*, Chapter 15 (results U1–U18).
- The evidence register, `tc/ch15/README.md` (revision 3), with each note's role, review status, source content hash and edition SHA-256.
- The preparation record, `tc/ch15/PREPARATION.md`, tracing each edition to its source file.
- Repository documentation in `docs/`.

**Scientific caveats**

- The notes were prepared with AI assistance under the author's direction. None has had external scientific review.
- Mathematics, numbers, verdicts and conclusions are as written in the cited working files, errors included. Later corrections, withdrawals and limits are flagged at the affected passage (26 editorial notes in 16 notes).
- WN-05, WN-24 and WN-28 are partly withdrawn or corrected by later notes and are released with those notes flagged.
- The formulas of WN-26 (result U10) are unverified.
- Calculation scripts, outputs and archives referred to in the notes are not included.
