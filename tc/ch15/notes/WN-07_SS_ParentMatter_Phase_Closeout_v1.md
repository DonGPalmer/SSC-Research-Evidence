# Scale Space parent-to-DYN matter universality — agreed phase closeout

**Date:** 8 November 2026. **Disposition:** SCIENTIFIC PHASE CLOSED; NONCANONICAL. The author agrees with the previous independent decision; this record does not promote a parent theory or amend governing documents.

## Decision

Retain the independent-leaf P2 matter construction only as a conditional **tree-level** candidate. No complete nonlinear reduction from the independent-leaf parent to the single universally coupled Jordan metric, no quantum radiative protection of the matter weights, and no distinctive gravitational observable are established. The finite common-metric SS-DYN v8 [doi:10.5281/zenodo.23862296](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23862296) action stays the accepted restricted model. The leading atomic Dirac–Maxwell bridge developed in a separate AI-assisted session remains a consistent matter completion of that *selected* metric, not an independently derived five-dimensional atom.

The completed independent calculations establish: (i) locked positive-weight leaf fields generally fail exact single-metric matching for unequal induced conformal factors; (ii) a nonzero orthogonal localizer source generates relative leaf-metric response in the finite graph; (iii) direct pure-longitudinal coupling to a conserved *complete* matter stress tensor cancels at leading order by a Ward identity, but that does not prove nonlinear metric equality; (iv) a three-leaf massive Fierz–Pauli model has exact protected common-mode overlap, with an ordinary relative-mode correction under unequal matter weights. These are bounded model calculations, not observed SS gravity.

WS1 P15 and P17 (working notes, not independently fully replayed here) raise radiative naturalness and heavy-mode threshold issues for a per-leaf scalar copy model. Full-tower loop sums are not by themselves cutoff-matched Wilsonian threshold calculations. Neither a universal no-go theorem nor a quantum protection theorem follows.

**Reopening rule:** require either a concrete symmetry/regulated parent that protects matter/gravity weights across thresholds or a fully constrained, nonlinearly consistent parent with one physical Jordan metric. The mechanism must first be independently validated. Otherwise no further parent-parameter scanning, new fitted matter couplings or canonical promotion.
