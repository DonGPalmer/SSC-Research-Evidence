# SS–MAT–DYN: Dirac–Maxwell parent-to-DYN universality test

**8 November 2026 · noncanonical independent derivation · no changes to canonical papers**

## Decision

**A necessary and sufficient exact metric-matching condition is derived for a specific locked Dirac–Maxwell subsector.** For identical positive per-leaf normalization weights and positive conformal factors, the kinetic, rest-mass and Maxwell coefficients can reproduce a **single minimally coupled conformal metric with the same bare mass, charge and overall normalization** if and only if the participating leaf conformal factors are identical. The obstruction is strictly positive when they differ; it starts at order *relative metric variance*, not at linear order. This provides a direct explanation of why the current WS1 scalar light-mode linear-protection tests cannot by themselves establish the full universal Dirac–Maxwell matter coupling used in `WS3_AtomicBridge_v1` (not included).

**Parent derivation not completed.** A dynamical constraint enforcing identical metrics and a fixed, universal matter normalization is not supplied by the live independent-leaf action reviewed here. Neither the necessary condition nor a finite-mass matching expansion is a no-go theorem for all possible parent completions. A microscopic profile choice, renormalized matching terms or an actual diagonal-metric constraint might instead supply an effective universal coupling, but these must be derived and controlled.

## Exact local Dirac–Maxwell matching lemma

Let independent four-dimensional leaf metrics be **conformal to the same comparison metric**,

\[
 g_{i\mu\nu}=A_i^2 g_{E\mu\nu},\quad A_i>0,\quad w_i>0.
\]

For every leaf take the same minimally coupled Dirac electron/proton field masses and charges and the same Maxwell field, and consider the diagnostic constrained *naively locked* fields, without a leaf-specific compensating wavefunction or parameter redefinition. Work at a point with locally constant `A_i` (this suffices for a necessary algebraic condition); derivative terms generated by varying conformal factors need independent control. Four-dimensional minimal matter gives the different conformal factors

\[
 \sqrt{-g_i}\,\overline\psi\gamma_i^\mu D_\mu\psi\sim A_i^3,
 \qquad \sqrt{-g_i}\,m\overline\psi\psi\sim A_i^4,
 \qquad \sqrt{-g_i}F_{i\mu\nu}F_i^{\mu\nu}\sim A_i^0.
\]

Define the *fixed* weighted moments

\[
 C_0=\sum_iw_i,\quad C_3=\sum_iw_iA_i^3,\quad C_4=\sum_iw_iA_i^4.
\]

For the **whole locked action** to equal one Dirac–Maxwell action `C0 S_m[A_eff² g_E, ψ; m,e]` with unchanged microscopic mass and charge, one must have

\[
 C_3=C_0 A_{\mathrm{eff}}^3,\qquad C_4=C_0 A_{\mathrm{eff}}^4,
 \qquad\Longrightarrow\quad \boxed{C_3^4=C_0 C_4^3}.
\]

Weighted Hölder or the power-mean inequality gives

\[
 \boxed{C_3^4\le C_0 C_4^3},
\]

with equality for strictly positive `w_i` **if and only if** all `A_i` are equal. In the equal-metric limit the normalized action is just the declared common Dirac–Maxwell action, and the later canonical SS-DYN transformation `g_E=Φ g_J` implies `g_J=Φ^{-1}g_E`.

**Do not overextend:** This is an exact test for the stated positive-weight, unrescaled common-Dirac-field reduction and *unchanged* bare parameters. If a microscopic parent allows leaf-dependent fermion profiles, frame-dependent field redefinitions, separate wavefunction renormalizations or specially matched higher operators, the reduction need not satisfy the naive moment equations; such a different construction requires a fresh calculation. It would not establish universal coupling merely by assigning effective masses after the fact.

## Nonlinear obstruction appears at quadratic order, despite linear protection

A two-leaf example with unit weights and symmetric relative conformal factors `A_±=A(1±δ)` has

\[
 C_0=2,\qquad C_3=2A^3(1+3\delta^2),\qquad
 C_4=2A^4(1+6\delta^2+\delta^4).
\]

At fixed comparison Einstein-frame kinetic normalization, the effective fermion mass multiplier is

\[
 \frac{C_4}{C_3}=A\frac{1+6\delta^2+\delta^4}{1+3\delta^2}
 =A[1+3\delta^2+O(\delta^4)].
\]

The conformal metric inferred from the Maxwell and fermion kinetic terms would be

\[
 A_{\rm eff}=A(1+3\delta^2)^{1/3},
\]

and its mass-term prediction differs from the actual mass term by

\[
 \frac{(C_4/C_0)}{(C_3/C_0)^{4/3}}
 =1+2\delta^2+O(\delta^4).
\]

The entire first-order effect vanishes by symmetry. The leading deviation is nonzero at second order. This is a **strictly stronger and directly relevant result** than the earlier massive-scalar `C0 C4−C2²` test because the atomic bridge specifically uses Dirac matter.

There is an additional **matter-equation obstruction**, already visible for a simple scalar comparator. With constant `A±`, naive leaf equations of the form `(A_±² □_E−A_±⁴m²)χ=0` differ by

\[
 4\delta[\Box_E-2m^2(1+\delta^2)]\chi=0.
\]

This difference generally is not satisfied by an arbitrary nontrivial common massive field. A finite hopping term can only create *approximate* locking after integrating out massive relative fields; a fully exact diagonal constraint must be established separately.

## Finite relative-mode integration: what can and cannot be concluded

If a stable relative mode `Y` has quadratic action schematically `L_rel=−(H/2)Y²+Y J_\mathrm{rel}` (where `H` is the positive massive operator in the relevant Euclidean/static subproblem), then

\[
 Y=H^{-1}J_{\rm rel},\quad
 \Delta L_{\rm eff}=\frac12J_{\rm rel}H^{-1}J_{\rm rel}.
\]

For long wavelengths and a mass gap `M_rel`, this produces local interactions beginning at schematic order `J_rel²/M_rel²`, with derivative corrections controlled by `E²/M_rel²` and relative-profile overlaps. **Even a suppressed relative field therefore need not leave a purely minimal common-metric matter action.** The tensor projectors, constraints, signs of Lorentzian Green functions and exact coefficients must come from a specified healthy parent; the simple scalar calculation does not constitute a completed multimetric spin-2 EFT.

For symmetry-protected matter backgrounds with `J_rel=0`, there is no such tree-level exchange at the tested order. That exception illustrates why the actual source projections matter; nothing here proves an unavoidable universality violation in every possible parent.

## Concrete next decision gate

To genuinely *derive* the atomic universal coupling from the SS parent, the parent builder would have to provide all of:

1. **Metric selection:** the exact equation, constraint or low-energy theorem making all participating light-matter induced vierbeins equal (or a demonstrated alternative universal effective vierbein with matching couplings), including corrections at second order in relative metric fluctuations.
2. **Matter reduction:** a controlled diagonal Dirac and Maxwell zero-mode profile, a heavy-mode gap, field/charge normalization and explicit integration-out errors, not just a linear scalar source profile.
3. **Localizer variation:** a fixed background protected overall normalization or an action-derived cancellation of its extra `W_{,I} L_m` scalar charge. Otherwise universality of the trace source fails even if the leaf metrics agree.
4. **Finite regulator and nonlinear constraints:** compatibility of the massive-relative sector with the parent lapse/shift constraints; the three-site rank calculation is evidence but not the whole secondary-constraint chain.
5. **Canonical reduction:** a demonstrated matching to the SS-DYN Jordan action `S_m[g_J,ψ]`, followed by the already-established Weyl transformation to `g_J=Φ^{-1}g_E`.

A positive answer to all five would supply a conditional **parent derivation of universal matter coupling**. Until then the atomic bridge is consistent with a selected physical matter metric but not compelled by the parent construction.
